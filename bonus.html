Connect your price to your income goal

Divide your monthly income goal by your offer price to estimate the clients needed. Include expenses and capacity when checking the plan.

Example
A $4,500 monthly revenue target at $1,500 per client requires three clients. At a 50% call-to-client conversion rate, that requires six qualified calls. Treat these as planning assumptions and track your actual results.

Choose your marketing focus

Choose a platform your audience uses. Share the problems you solve, examples of your work, and a clear next step.

Set a posting schedule you can maintain. Track inquiries and calls to see which activity produces interest.

Plan direct outreach

List 10–15 people who fit your audience or could introduce you. Send each a personal message explaining your offer and asking about their needs or a relevant introduction.

Example message
“I’m offering [service] for [audience] dealing with [problem]. Do you know someone who could use that help?”

Follow up once after seven days if needed. Use your intake questions with interested people, and ask satisfied clients for referrals.
