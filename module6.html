// Part 1 — Scope Agreement Template

These terms go into every client agreement. Use plain language.

Project scope (what you are agreeing to do)
Reference your offer document from Module 4. Specific deliverables, sessions, and timeline.
What is NOT included
Copy from your Module 4 exclusions. These must be explicit.
Client responsibilities
What the client must provide. Late or missing information = paused timeline.
Revision policy
How many rounds are included and what happens when that limit is reached.
Payment terms
Deposit, final payment, late fees.
Cancellation policy
What happens if either party ends early.
