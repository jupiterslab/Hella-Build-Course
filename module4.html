// Module 04 — Phase R: Revenue

Build Your Offer

Define your deliverables, scope, timeline, price, and completion criteria.

⏱ ~25 minutes
📐 Offer Blueprint Worksheet
🔒 Lock-In: Your signature offer description
Define the offer

An offer tells a client what they receive, what it costs, and when it will be complete.

Example
A 30-day positioning project includes an audience profile, offer statement, and messaging guide. Define the deliverables, revisions, exclusions, and completion criteria.

Set and explain your price

Consider the value of the outcome, comparable offers, delivery costs, and time required. Choose a price that covers the work and supports your income goal.

Record the reasoning so you can explain the price and review it as your costs and experience change.

Respond to pricing questions

Ask what the client needs clarified. Explain the deliverables, expected outcome, and terms.

Example response
“The fee covers [deliverables] over [timeline]. Which part would you like me to clarify?” If the budget is lower, discuss a smaller scope you can deliver at that price.
